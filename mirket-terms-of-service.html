2. Description of Service

Mirket is a posture companion. It uses the head-tracking sensors in compatible AirPods and headphones to estimate the forward tilt of your head, shows it with an animated character, and gives gentle cues (a chime, a vibration or a notification) when your head stays forward for a while. It also offers short neck routines, movement breaks and progress summaries.

Not medical advice: Mirket is a wellness and awareness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and the angles and neck-load figures it shows are estimates. If you have pain or a health concern, talk to a qualified healthcare professional. Do the neck routines gently and stop if anything hurts.
